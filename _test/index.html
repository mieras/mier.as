About

I'm a designer who codes, or a developer who designs—depends on who's asking. I turn coffee into pixels and pixels into problems. My superpower? Making things look easy while secretly debugging at 3 AM. I believe in design systems, semantic HTML, and that one perfect gradient that took three hours to get right.

Music

When I'm not wrestling with CSS, I'm playing records that would make my neighbors question their life choices. Ambient soundscapes for coding, jungle for debugging, and lo-fi hip-hop for when the build finally works. My music is like my code: experimental, sometimes broken, but always interesting.
